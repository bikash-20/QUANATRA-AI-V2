# Word Problems

- distance = rate x time. Round trip average speed = total distance / total time (harmonic, not arithmetic).
- Work: rates add. 1/6 + 1/12 = 1/4 job per hour, so 4 hours.
- Compound growth: P (1 + r)^t.
- Mixtures: amount of solute is conserved: sum(conc x volume) = final conc x final volume.
